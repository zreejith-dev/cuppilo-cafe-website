About CUPPILO

Cupillo is a 1-year research campaign to build Kerala's first community-built specialty café. We're not selling coffee yet — we're building the menu, ambiance, and pricing with you.

Every response shapes what Cuppilo becomes. Your vote on drinks, food, atmosphere, and pricing directly influences our concept.
